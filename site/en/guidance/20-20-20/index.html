The 20-20-20 guide

After 20 minutes,
look a little farther.

20-20-20 is an easy-to-remember screen-break habit, not a diagnosis, treatment or promise of results.

20Every 20 minutes
20Look about 20 feet away
20For about 20 seconds

What does 20-20-20 mean?

The American Optometric Association (AOA) describes a general habit: every 20 minutes of screen use, spend 20 seconds looking at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away. It is a memorable rhythm, not a precise prescription that works identically for everyone.

How can I fit it into a workday?

  1. Choose a distant point, such as a tree outside or the end of a hallway; do not simply switch to your phone.
  2. When a reminder arrives, look away for about 20 seconds, blink naturally and adjust your posture.
  3. Also consider your environment: reduce glare, keep a comfortable screen distance and make room for longer breaks away from the screen.

What can Breather do — and not do?

You can set a break rhythm that suits you, such as a 20-minute interval and a 20-second break. The app provides reminders and computer activity estimates. It cannot know whether you actually looked into the distance, does not measure eye fatigue and has not demonstrated prevention or treatment of disease. Its fatigue indicator is only a reminder model.

Persistent symptoms need more than reminders

If eye pain, blurred vision or other symptoms persist, consult an eye-care professional. Sudden or severe changes in vision need prompt medical attention. This page is general educational information, not a substitute for professional advice.
