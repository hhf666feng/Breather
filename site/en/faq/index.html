Frequently asked questions

A few things worth knowing.

Straight answers about time estimates, reminders, platforms and product limits.

What is Breather?

Breather is a desktop eye break reminder. It estimates use from computer activity signals and reminds you to pause on your chosen schedule. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat eye conditions.

Does computer-use time measure actual gaze?

No. Idle detection only supplies approximate computer activity signals; it cannot prove human presence or gaze. Quiet reading, video playback and background animation can differ from actual screen viewing. Optional enhanced detection does not remove this limit.

Is the fatigue indicator a medical measurement?

No. The fatigue indicator is a reminder model calculated from work and break durations, not a measurement of eye strain, eyesight or health. It is not clinically validated and should not guide decisions about seeking care.

Which devices are supported? Is there an iPhone app?

macOS 1.6.2 supports Apple Silicon on macOS 13+. Windows 1.6.0 is an x64 Electron 45.3.0 app. There is no iPhone app, Intel Mac release package or native Windows ARM64 package.

Is it free? Do I need a subscription or account?

The current desktop releases are free to download, with no subscription or app account required. Free use does not mean the repository carries an open-source license.

Will my Mac and Windows records sync automatically?

No. Settings and records are local, with no cloud autosync. You can export backups manually. Do not assume backup formats are interchangeable across platforms or versions; keep original data before restoring.

Which permissions are used? Are typed keys recorded?

Optional macOS enhanced input detection uses Accessibility permission and activity timestamps, not retained key text. Optional screen activity detection requires Screen Recording permission and compares low-resolution grayscale grids in memory without saving screen images. Windows uses system idle time and does not provide the same macOS enhanced detection.

Why does my system show a security warning?

Current packages are unsigned and the Mac app is not notarized, so Gatekeeper or SmartScreen may warn. Verify the official source and do not bypass protections when uncertain.

Does 20-20-20 guarantee relief?

No. It is general screen-break guidance, and Breather makes no claim of proven medical benefit. Persistent eye pain, blurred vision or other symptoms deserve advice from an eye-care professional.
