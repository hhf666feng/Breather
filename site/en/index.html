Not more to do.
A moment to step away.

Breather turns computer activity signals into readable time estimates. It is a habit tool, not a gaze tracker, and its numbers are not a medical assessment.

01

See your rhythm

Review estimated computer-use time and local history. Idle and activity signals are approximations, not proof that you are looking at the screen.

02

Make space for breaks

Set break intervals and duration around your work. When a reminder arrives, give something farther away a little attention.

03

Local by default. Backup by choice.

Data is local by default. Mac users can opt into an automatic backup folder; the app updates EyeWatch-backup.json about every 30 seconds and on key actions. If iCloud Drive is selected, the operating system may sync that file automatically. There is no built-in account, CloudKit cross-device merging or iPhone app.

Give your eyes
a different distance.

The 20-20-20 rule is a simple screen-break habit: every 20 minutes, look about 20 feet (6 metres) away for 20 seconds. Start by remembering to pause.
